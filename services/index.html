Treatments

Modern dental service overview

Each service includes the usual procedure, timing, visit expectations and aftercare guidance. Final advice depends on clinical examination.

Dental Checkup

A complete oral examination to catch concerns early and plan the right care.

Treatment procedure

  • Medical history review
  • Teeth, gums and bite examination
  • X-ray advice if needed

Who needs it

Anyone with tooth pain, sensitivity, bleeding gums or a routine six-month visit.

Benefits

Early diagnosis, clearer treatment planning and better prevention.

Timing

Duration: 20-30 minutes

Healing/Cure time: No healing time

Visits: 1 visit

Consultation: 10-15 minutes

Aftercare tips: Brush normally and follow any review advice.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove plaque, tartar and stains.

Treatment procedure

  • Ultrasonic scaling
  • Polishing
  • Gum care guidance

Who needs it

Patients with deposits, stains, bad breath or gum bleeding.

Benefits

Fresher breath, healthier gums and a cleaner smile.

Timing

Duration: 30-45 minutes

Healing/Cure time: Mild sensitivity may settle in 1-2 days

Visits: 1 visit

Consultation: 10 minutes

Aftercare tips: Avoid very hot, cold or colored foods for a few hours.

Tooth Filling

Tooth-colored restoration for cavities, chipped teeth or small fractures.

Treatment procedure

  • Decay removal
  • Tooth cleaning
  • Composite filling and shaping

Who needs it

Small cavities, food lodgement or broken tooth edges.

Benefits

Restores tooth shape, comfort and chewing function.

Timing

Duration: 30-45 minutes

Healing/Cure time: Usually immediate

Visits: 1 visit

Consultation: 10 minutes

Aftercare tips: Avoid biting hard foods until numbness wears off.

Root Canal Treatment

Treatment to save an infected or painful tooth instead of removing it.

Treatment procedure

  • Diagnosis and X-ray
  • Canal cleaning
  • Disinfection and sealing
  • Crown planning when required

Who needs it

Severe toothache, swelling, deep cavity or lingering sensitivity.

Benefits

Pain relief, infection control and natural tooth preservation.

Timing

Duration: 45-90 minutes per sitting

Healing/Cure time: Tenderness may settle in 2-5 days

Visits: 1-3 visits

Consultation: 15 minutes

Aftercare tips: Do not chew hard foods on the treated tooth until restored.

Dental Crown

A custom cap to protect and strengthen a weak or root canal treated tooth.

Treatment procedure

  • Tooth preparation
  • Digital or physical impression
  • Temporary crown
  • Final crown fixing

Who needs it

Root canal treated teeth, cracked teeth or heavily filled teeth.

Benefits

Improved strength, appearance and chewing comfort.

Timing

Duration: 30-60 minutes each visit

Healing/Cure time: Gum tenderness may settle in 1-3 days

Visits: 2 visits

Consultation: 10-15 minutes

Aftercare tips: Keep margins clean and avoid cracking hard items.

Dental Bridge

A fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth supported by nearby teeth.

Treatment procedure

  • Assessment
  • Support tooth preparation
  • Impression
  • Bridge trial and fixing

Who needs it

Patients missing teeth with healthy support teeth nearby.

Benefits

Restores chewing, smile continuity and speech support.

Timing

Duration: 45-75 minutes each visit

Healing/Cure time: 1-3 days for gum comfort

Visits: 2-3 visits

Consultation: 15 minutes

Aftercare tips: Clean under the bridge as advised.

Dental Implant

A stable tooth replacement option using an implant-supported crown.

Treatment procedure

  • 3D/X-ray planning
  • Implant placement
  • Healing phase
  • Crown attachment

Who needs it

Adults with missing teeth and suitable bone support.

Benefits

Natural feel, strong chewing support and no trimming of adjacent teeth.

Timing

Duration: 45-90 minutes surgery

Healing/Cure time: Usually 3-6 months integration

Visits: 3-5 visits

Consultation: 20 minutes

Aftercare tips: Maintain excellent cleaning and attend reviews.

Tooth Extraction

Careful removal of a damaged, loose or non-restorable tooth.

Treatment procedure

  • Examination and X-ray if needed
  • Local anesthesia
  • Gentle extraction
  • Post-care instructions

Who needs it

Broken, infected, mobile or badly decayed teeth that cannot be saved.

Benefits

Removes infection source and relieves pain.

Timing

Duration: 20-45 minutes

Healing/Cure time: Soft tissue healing usually 7-10 days

Visits: 1 visit plus review if needed

Consultation: 10-15 minutes

Aftercare tips: Bite on gauze, avoid spitting and follow diet guidance.
Book Appointment

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Planned removal of painful, impacted or repeatedly infected wisdom teeth.

Treatment procedure

  • Clinical exam
  • X-ray planning
  • Surgical or simple removal
  • Review when required

Who needs it

Pain, swelling, jaw stiffness or food trapping near wisdom teeth.

Benefits

Prevents repeated infection and protects nearby teeth.

Timing

Duration: 30-75 minutes

Healing/Cure time: About 7-14 days depending on complexity

Visits: 1-2 visits

Consultation: 15-20 minutes

Aftercare tips: Use cold compresses, soft food and prescribed medicines.

Patients missing several or all teeth.

Benefits

Improves chewing, speech and facial support.

Braces

Fixed orthodontic treatment for crowded teeth, gaps and bite correction.

Treatment procedure

  • Orthodontic assessment
  • Records and plan
  • Bracket placement
  • Regular adjustments

Who needs it

Children, teens or adults with alignment or bite concerns.

Benefits

Straighter teeth, improved bite and easier cleaning.

Timing

Healing/Cure time: Mild soreness after adjustments for 2-4 days

Aligners

Clear removable trays for discreet smile alignment in suitable cases.

Treatment procedure

  • Scan or impressions
  • Treatment simulation
  • Tray delivery
  • Progress reviews

Who needs it

Mild to moderate crowding, spacing or relapse after braces.

Benefits

Nearly invisible, removable and comfortable for daily life.

Timing

Healing/Cure time: Mild pressure for 1-3 days per tray

Visits: Periodic review visits
